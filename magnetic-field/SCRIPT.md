# What If Earth Lost Its Magnetic Field? — script, draft 1

**Brief**
- **Audience:** teens and a general audience.
- **Length:** about 64 s (60.2 s of narration after trimming). Every beat answers the question the last one raised.
- **Tone:** plain, friendly, no slang.
- **Voice:** edge-tts "Ryan" (`en-GB-RyanNeural`, rate +5%), one take per line, made with the shared `tools/voice.py`,
  which trims the dead air edge-tts leaves around and inside each take. Lines are 0.2 s apart in the timeline, so the
  read flows (about 0.35 s of silence between sentences, down from 1.2–1.7 s in *What If the Sun Vanished?*).

**Why this topic:** the channel's best Shorts are "what if something big changed about our planet" (the Moon, the
rings, Earth stopping, the Sun vanishing). This one stars both characters: the lazy Sun blowing its wind at us and
Earth's invisible shield. The surprise: on the ground you'd mostly be fine, the real danger is a slow leak, and
Mars shows how it ends.

**Title:** "What If Earth Lost Its Magnetic Field?" · **Hook (first 3 s):** the shield switches off and the compass gives up.
**First frame (no sound):** Earth inside dashed cyan field loops, big text **WHAT IF EARTH LOST ITS MAGNETIC FIELD?**
**Thumbnail (designed separately):** a scared Earth, its shield shattering, solar wind pouring in, a lost compass, "NO SHIELD".
