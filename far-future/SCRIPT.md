# "The Next 100 Billion Years, in 90 Seconds" — script, draft 1

**Channel:** Beyond Everything. **Style:** our brand flat-vector look (same characters, palette, sparkle stars and gold captions as the Moon and Lazy Sun films). Vertical 1080×1920, about 92 s.

**Voice:** Ryan (UK) at +12%, the same as The Sun Is Lazy. A narration preview is in [narration-preview.mp3](narration-preview.mp3), and speech-to-text reads every line back correctly.

**The device:** a big gold **time odometer** sits at the top of every scene, starting at the current year. Before each jump, the Earth character presses a giant ⏩ fast-forward button. The counter spins with a whoosh, everything blurs, and we land in the new era. The jumps get bigger and the counter has to grow extra digits, and that escalation is the hook. Recurring characters (the Earth, the Moon, the lazy Sun) carry the jokes.

**Length:** about 250 words, about 92 s with pauses.

**Why it should hold attention:**
- **The hook promises a huge payoff in a fixed time,** and the counter on screen keeps score.
- **Every jump is bigger than the last,** and the counter's digits keep growing, which makes the progress satisfying to watch.
- **Each step has a visual joke:** Polaris late with its coffee, Orion's shrug, Mars's mirror, the PANGAEA 2 poster, and the lazy Sun finally working.
- **The ending is the strongest twist:** the universe goes dark, then a warm "enjoy it now" in the last line.
