# The Next 100 Billion Years — v2 (story-first, about 65 s)

**Title:** "One Day the Night Sky Will Be Empty". **First frame (no sound):** today's starry sky with galaxies, under big text **"THIS SKY WILL DISAPPEAR"**, and Earth looking up, worried.
**What changed from v1 (98 s → 65 s):** the hook is now a mystery (an empty sky) that the 100-billion-year ending pays off, instead of "the next 100 billion years in 90 seconds". Every stop is one short line. The 30M and 100M stops merge into one Mars/Saturn joke. "But that's not the strange part" keeps viewers watching from one billion years onwards. All nine milestones you asked for are still there, plus 250M, which became the Pangaea joke in v1 and is cut here for pace.
