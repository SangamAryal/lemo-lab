# What If the Sun Vanished? — script, draft 1

**Brief**
- **Audience:** teens and a general audience.
- **Length:** about 66 s (64.5 s of narration). Every beat answers the question the last one raised.
- **Tone:** plain, friendly, no slang.
- **Voice:** edge-tts "Ryan" (`en-GB-RyanNeural`, rate +5%), one take per line, the house voice for Beyond Everything. Rebuild with `sh build.sh --vo`.

**Why this topic:** the channel's best Shorts are "what if something big changed about our planet or sky" (the Moon, Earth's rings, Earth stopping). This is the biggest one of all, it stars the lazy Sun, and the hook is a real surprise: you wouldn't notice for eight minutes. It's different from *Alpha Centauri* (the Sun swapped for another star) and *Far Future* (the Sun dying slowly).

**Title:** "What If the Sun Vanished?" · **Hook (first 2 s):** "you wouldn't know for eight minutes."
**First frame (no sound):** the lazy Sun vanishing in a "POOF" with only its shades left in mid-air, Earth still smiling and lit, and big text **WHAT IF THE SUN VANISHED?**
**Thumbnail (designed separately):** the empty POOF with the floating shades, a worried Earth, big gold "8:20".

## Choices
- **"Eight minutes"** in the hook and the ending; the exact 8 min 20 s (499 s) is said once, at the blackout.
- **Jupiter "for over an hour":** the light has to go Sun → Jupiter (about 43 min) and then Jupiter → Earth (35–55 min), so it keeps arriving for roughly 70–90 minutes after our Sun goes dark.
- **Not claimed:** how long people would survive, or exactly when the deep ocean would freeze. The sources give only rough guesses.
- **Temperatures** are said as "about" and "on average", because they are rough model estimates.
