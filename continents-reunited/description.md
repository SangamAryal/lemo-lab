**Title:** What If All Continents Reunited? 🌍

**Description:**
Every continent on Earth is moving, and one day they'll all crash back together. It's happened before: about 300 million years ago almost all land was one supercontinent, Pangaea. That's why the same little reptile, Mesosaurus, is found in both Brazil and South Africa.

So what if they reunited? You could drive from New York to London, and ships, which carry about 80% of world trade today, would give way to trucks and trains. The middle of the supercontinent would be farther from the sea than anywhere on Earth, with scorching summers, freezing winters and giant deserts. Pangaea had them: its dunes turned to stone and became the red cliffs of Zion National Park. The coasts would get megamonsoons, the one giant ocean would have less shallow sea for sea life, and colliding continents would push up mountains as tall as the Himalayas.

Don't panic: continents move about as fast as your fingernails grow, so the next supercontinent is about 250 million years away. One 2023 study found it could be so hot that mammals could only live on about a tenth of it. And Earth has done this at least three times before.

Which city would you road-trip to first? 👇
