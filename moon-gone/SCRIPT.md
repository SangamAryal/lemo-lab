**Look:** our flat Moon-Breakup characters in 2.5D. Every beat is shown **where it happens**: on the beach, in a tide pool, underwater on the reef, on someone's street at night. We only go to space when the idea really is in space. Each row is tagged with its point of view.
**Sound** (from memory): about one SFX per second, whoosh→boom pairs on reveals, a steady mellow music bed with an outro swell, and meme SFX on the joke beats.

## Analysis of the draft

**What works:**
- The hook: "the Moon is gone" sounds impossible.
- The Gen Z voice: "I'm out", "are we cooked", "take an L", "not a vibe", "Bro's been carrying Earth".
- The structure: oceans → life → a long-term threat → a reassurance → a payoff.

**What to fix:**
1. **Too long.** The draft is 299 words, about 125 s of speech plus pauses, so roughly 2:15, over the 120 s cap. The rewrite is 234 words, about 107 s.
2. **AI-sounding phrases.** "Imagine waking up…" (a banned opener), "You see,", "But wait, there's more" (a formula), "And that's where things get messy".
3. **Lines too long to say in one breath.** "Weaker tides mean weaker tidal currents, which could change how nutrients move through coastal waters" (17 words, plus jargon) and the coral sentence.
4. **Jargon.** "Nutrients", "ecosystems", "synchronize", "lunar cues" → "food", "sea life", "pick one night", "moonlight".
5. **A fact made vaguer than it needs to be.** "Much weaker in many places" → "less than half their size" (the Sun's tide is about 46% of the Moon's).
6. **A missing visual beat.** The draft says nights would be darker but never shows it. It's now its own line, a strong ground-level visual.
7. **The ending summary** ("our nights would become much darker, our oceans would change…") repeats what we already said, so it's cut. The payoff line is stronger on its own.

## Fact checks

| Claim | Source / check | Hedge |
|---|---|---|
| The Moon makes most of Earth's tides | The Moon's tidal force is about 2.2× the Sun's (NOAA). | — |
| Sun-only tides would be less than half their current size | The solar tide is about 46% of the lunar tide (NOAA Tides & Currents). | "less than half" |
| Weaker tides → weaker coastal currents → less nutrient mixing | Tidal currents mix coastal waters; general oceanography. | "could" |
| Intertidal animals could lose feeding and breeding habitat | The intertidal zone is set by the tidal range. | "could" |
| Corals use moonlight to time mass spawning | Great Barrier Reef mass spawning comes a few nights after a spring full moon; moonlight is a known cue (Kaniewska et al. 2015, among others). | "many corals" |
| A full Moon is about 100× brighter than starlight | Full moon about 0.1–0.3 lux; moonless clear sky about 0.001–0.002 lux. | "around" |
| The Moon stabilises Earth's tilt | Laskar et al. 1993: without the Moon, the tilt could vary chaotically. Later work (Lissauer et al. 2012) suggests the swings may be smaller and slower. | "could wobble a lot more, over millions of years" |
| Earth wouldn't fly off or stop spinning | Earth orbits the Sun, not the Moon, and its spin doesn't depend on the Moon. | — |
