**Brief**
- Audience: general Shorts viewers, about 14 and up
- Length: about 60–68 s per part, including short pauses between depth beats
- Tone: a friend telling you something strange, calm and curious, no hype
- Voice: en-GB-RyanNeural. **Change the rate from +12% to +4%** and add a 0.3–0.4 s pause before each new depth. The old scripts were 171 and 172 words at about 2.6 words/s. These are 156 and 160 words at about 2.4 words/s, so the narration is slower and the runtime stays about the same.
