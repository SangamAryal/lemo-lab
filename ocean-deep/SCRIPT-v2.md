# Journey to the Bottom of the Ocean — v2, two parts (about 66 s + 69 s)

**Why two parts:** you asked for every detail of the reference video, and that's about 2 min 40 s in one Short, which few people finish. Two hooked parts keep every fact, double the chances to be recommended, and Part 1's cliffhanger ("Part two") pushes viewers to the next one and to subscribe. Every reference fact is still in one of the two parts (FACTS.md is unchanged). The only small cuts: Cameron's sub name ("Deepsea Challenger" goes on screen), the Kaiko years (on screen), and "thousands of undiscovered species" (now implied by "explored just 5–10%").

**The device:** the yellow submarine character dives; the depth gauge shows **metres and feet**. The water darkens zone by zone and the headlight switches on at 1,000 m.
