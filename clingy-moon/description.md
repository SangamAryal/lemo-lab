**Title:** Mars Has a Clingy Moon 💍

**Description:**
Our Moon is slowly breaking up with us. Mars has the opposite problem. 🔴

Phobos orbits just 6,000 km above Mars, laps it three times a day, and creeps about 2 m closer every century. In 30–50 million years Mars will tear it apart, and the pieces could become a ring. Meanwhile Deimos is quietly drifting away. And their names? Fear and Dread. Red flags. 🚩

Would you rather have a moon that leaves you, or one that never lets go?

Sources: NASA Science (Phobos, Deimos), NASA Goddard (2015, "Phobos is falling apart"), NASA/JPL Perseverance eclipse (2022), Black & Mittal, Nature Geoscience (2015).
