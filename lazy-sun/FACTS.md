## Checked and left out
- **"Light from the Sun is older than the pyramids / farming."** The low estimate (10,000 years) is younger than farming (about 12,000 years), so the line says "thousands of years — maybe tens of thousands". The visual montage only *passes* the pyramids; it doesn't claim the photon is older than them.
- **"You are brighter than the Sun".** That's only true per volume and only for heat (infrared), so the script says "more heat per cubic metre".
