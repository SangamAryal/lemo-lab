The Moon is slowly moving away from us — about 3.8 cm every single year 🌙📏
That's roughly as fast as your fingernails grow.

We know this because astronauts left mirrors on the Moon, and scientists still bounce lasers off them to measure the distance.

In ~600 million years, it'll look too small to fully cover the Sun — no more total solar eclipses. 😢
