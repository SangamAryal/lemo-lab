# What If Earth Stopped Spinning? — script, draft 1

**Brief**
- **Audience:** teens and a general audience.
- **Length:** about 80 s. That's over 60 s but well under the 120 s limit; every beat answers the last question.
- **Tone:** plain, friendly, no slang.
- **Voice:** Ryan (UK) +5%, the Earth Rings voice. Preview: [narration-preview.mp3](narration-preview.mp3), and speech-to-text reads it back correctly.

**Why this topic:** your top two Shorts are both "What if something big changed about our own planet or sky" (the Moon at 2.7K, Earth's rings at 2.3K). This one follows the same pattern. Its ending also points back to the Moon video: the Moon is what's slowing Earth down.

**Title:** "What If Earth Stopped Spinning?" **Thumbnail (designed separately):** a cartoon person and their house mid-air over a city, with Earth behind them wearing a "STOP" sign, and big text "1,600 km/h".

**Timing:** 211 words, about 80 s recorded at Ryan +5%. That's within the channel's ≤120 s rule. If you want it shorter, the easiest cuts are line 13 (the magnetic field, −5 s) and the second half of line 11 (−3 s).

## Changes from the research
- **Cut:** your weight at the equator rising about 0.3% without the spin. It's true, but too small to matter for the story.
- **Not claimed:** wave heights, a death toll, or "everyone dies". Sources don't give solid numbers, so the script only says the oceans would crash over the land.
- **Wind wording:** says "across most of the planet", because near the poles the ground barely moves, so the wind there would be weak.
